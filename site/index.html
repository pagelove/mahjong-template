A MOMENT TO GET ACQUAINTED

One pair at a time.

  1. Find a matching pair.

    Choose two tiles with the same symbol. Tap quickly or use two fingers: each tile selects when you lift its finger. Slide to scroll a larger board; pinch to zoom. Flowers and winds match their exact symbol, too.

  2. Give your tiles some space.

    A tile needs an open left or right side, with no tile covering it. Dimmed tiles are blocked. Tap one to see why.

  3. Clear the garden.

    Remove every tile to finish. If you run out of pairs, undo a move, reshuffle or restart. There is no time limit.

A little help is always here.

Hints reveal an open pair. Undo restores your last move. A reshuffle creates a fresh solvable arrangement of the tiles left.

Earn one star for clearing, two without reshuffling, and three without hints or reshuffling. Points: 10,000 for a clean clear; hints deduct ceil(4,000 × hints ÷ original pairs), reshuffles deduct 2,000 each. Completion has a 1,000-point floor. Time does not affect rank. Undo has no penalty or point farming. Your progress and photos stay in this browser.

    How scoring works

    A clean clear earns 10,000 points. Hints deduct a share of 4,000 based on the board's original pair count. Each reshuffle costs 2,000. Completion earns at least 1,000. Undo is free and does not rewind time. Faster active play adds a bonus of up to 10% of those base points; bigger boards get proportionally more time. A clean 12-pair clear earns 10,666 at 60s, 10,500 at 120s, or 10,333 at 240s. There is no deadline. Menus and hidden pages pause the timer. Existing recorded times use the same bonus rules.

    Overall adds the best time-adjusted score on each board. Replays count once per board. Matching names combine results without regard to case; names are public aliases. Equal totals share a rank. History is excluded.
